# Installing Pa-Nashe Tracker on your phone

The app lives at **https://oupamunashe.github.io/pa-nashe-tracker/**. Installing it puts the half-and-half dot on your home screen and opens it full screen, like any other app. It works offline too.

## iPhone (Safari)

1. Open the link above in **Safari** (other browsers on iPhone can't install apps).
2. Sign in with your email and password.
3. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
5. Open **Pa-Nashe** from your home screen. Sign in once more if it asks – the home-screen app keeps its own sign-in.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open the link above in **Chrome**.
2. Sign in with your email and password.
3. Tap the **⋮** menu (top right) and choose **Install app** (on some phones: **Add to Home screen → Install**).
4. Open **Pa-Nashe** from your home screen or app drawer.

## Good to know

- **Offline:** you can capture spends without a connection. The top bar shows “Offline – N changes waiting”; they are sent by themselves when you're back online. Keep the app open for a moment once you have signal again. Receipt photos need a connection.
- **Updates** install in the background and apply the next time you open the app.
- **Who is paying:** new entries default to whoever is signed in. *More → Settings → This device* changes the default.
- **Forgot your password?** Tap *Forgot password?* on the sign-in screen and follow the email.
- **Signing out** (*More → Settings → Sign out*) removes the tracker's data from that phone. If it says changes haven't been sent yet, go online first.
- **Backups:** *More → Download a full backup* saves everything as one file – worth doing now and then.
